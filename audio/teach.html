Your name

Get an alert when someone says your name. Type it as people say it, plus any nicknames. Using the companion? Set your name in its Preferences instead: it pushes its saved name to the receiver whenever it connects. Recordings made here are kept as long as the name stays the same.

Then have a friend say just your name, 3 times. This teaches the model how your name sounds, which helps a lot for uncommon names.
